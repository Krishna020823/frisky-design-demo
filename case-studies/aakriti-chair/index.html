Case Study

Amazon Listing Images & Infographics | Premium Chair

A premium Amazon listing image system created for a handcrafted wooden chair. The visual direction combines photorealistic product presentation, lifestyle storytelling and conversion-focused infographics to highlight craftsmanship, comfort, materials and dimensions while maintaining a consistent e-commerce identity.

From Raw Product Shots to Conversion-Ready Listing Images

We transformed the supplied chair photographs into a cohesive Amazon visual system combining premium lifestyle imagery, benefit-led infographics and detail-focused storytelling to build trust, communicate value and help turn product views into purchases.

Amazon Listing Images — Amazon Graphics

Amazon listing images are the first impression your customers have of your product. These images allow customers to quickly view the appearance and features of your product, which can result in an immediate positive or negative response.

That's why it's crucial to optimize your images with high-quality, professional product and lifestyle shots, as well as Amazon infographics.

Well-designed listing images have been shown to increase the conversion rate, build brand awareness, and boost sales for your products.
