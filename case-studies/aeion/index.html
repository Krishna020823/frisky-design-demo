Case Study

Aeion

A full Amazon listing and A+ content set for a stainless-steel pressure cooker range — built to explain engineering in the few seconds a shopper gives a product page.

The Brief

A pressure cooker is a considered purchase. Shoppers compare safety, capacity and build before they buy, and they do it by scrolling images rather than reading copy. Aeion needed a listing set that answered those questions visually, in order, without ever feeling like a spec sheet.

Listing Images

The sequence opens on a clean hero shot, then works through the things that actually decide the sale — the triply base with its SS-304 lining, the four-layer safety system, the inner measurement scale, the gasket release and the extra locking mechanism. Each frame carries one idea, so the story still lands for anyone swiping quickly on a phone.

A+ Content

Below the fold, the A+ modules slow the pace down: lifestyle photography on a real hob, compatibility across gas and induction, and the non-toxic cookware claim given room to breathe. The layout keeps a consistent blue-and-white system throughout so the brand reads as one voice from the first thumbnail to the last module.

Engineering, made shoppable.
