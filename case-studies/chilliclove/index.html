Case Study

ChilliClove

A modern spice brand built to celebrate authentic Indian flavours with a bold visual identity and shelf-ready packaging system.

The Brief

ChilliClove was created as a modern spice brand that celebrates authentic flavours with a bold visual identity. The project spanned both logo design and a cohesive packaging system, built to create a memorable shelf presence in a highly competitive FMCG market.

The challenge was to stand out while still communicating freshness, quality and authenticity at first glance — so every design decision was built around three core principles: bold, authentic, memorable.

Logo Design

The logo captures the vibrant personality of the brand through clean forms and confident typography — an identity that's distinctive, timeless and instantly recognisable, whether shown in colour or reversed.

Packaging Design

Designed for maximum shelf impact, the packaging combines bold colours, clear hierarchy and appetising visuals so customers can identify products quickly — while a unified design language of consistent layout, vibrant palette and premium typography keeps the whole SKU range feeling like one brand.

Brand Applications

The identity extends naturally beyond the shelf — into stationery, merchandise and digital touchpoints — so the brand feels just as considered wherever it shows up.
