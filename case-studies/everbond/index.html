Case Study

Everbond

Brand identity and packaging for Everbond's paints, putty and construction cement range — built for trust on the shelf and on the job site.

The Brief

Everbond sits across three product lines — Abson Hi-Gloss exterior paint, Wall Shakti Putty, and Wall Shakti Construction & Decorative White Cement — each needing its own shelf identity while still reading as one dependable parent brand. The goal was packaging that feels premium and trustworthy at a glance, whether it's sitting in a hardware store or on a construction site.

Packaging Design

Across the putty and cement lines, a consistent structure and colour language keeps every bag instantly identifiable as Everbond, while clear product naming and iconography help customers pick the right product fast.

Abson Hi-Gloss Paint

For the paint line, packaging needed to work just as hard on a factory floor as it does convincing a homeowner mid-renovation — so we paired clean product photography with a simple before-and-after story that sells the finish, not just the can.

Brand in Use

The identity was designed to hold up in the field — from application shots to outdoor, real-world conditions.

Built to Last. Designed to Trust.
