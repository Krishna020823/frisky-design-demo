FAQ

Before you apply.

Is the Academy fully online?+
Yes — every class is Live Online, not pre-recorded video, so you get real-time instruction and personal mentor feedback throughout.
How long is the program?+
6 months, structured into six monthly modules — from Design Foundations through to Portfolio & Career.
Will I get a certificate?+
Yes, a certificate is awarded on successful completion of the program.
Is there an internship opportunity?+
Yes — strong performers get an Internship Opportunity as a pathway into real studio project work.
How many seats are open for Founding Batch 01?+
Just 20 seats. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, so earlier applicants get first access.
What will I have by graduation?+
8–10 portfolio projects, a professional brand identity, packaging design, a marketing campaign, e-commerce assets, a portfolio website, your certificate and career guidance.
