Privacy Policy

Frisky Designs ("we", "us", "our") is an independent branding and creative studio based in India, working with clients worldwide. This policy explains what information we collect through friskydesigns.com, why we collect it, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We only collect information that you choose to share with us, through the forms on this site:

  • Project Enquiry form (Contact page): name, email address, contact number, company/brand name, and project details you provide — such as business description, target audience, industry, colour and style preferences, budget range, timeline and any files or links you reference.
  • Academy Application form (Apply page): name, email address, phone number, city, date of birth (optional) and your responses about your background and goals.

We use a visitor analytics script (tawk.to, listed below) to understand how many people visit the site and which pages they read. It sets cookies in your browser and records technical information such as your IP address, approximate location, browser and the pages you view. We do not use advertising or marketing tracking pixels, and we do not require you to create an account to browse the site.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry or application and discuss your project or admission to the Academy.
  • To prepare proposals, quotes, and project or course-related communication.
  • To send you information you've specifically requested, such as the Academy curriculum.
  • To improve our services and understand what prospective clients and students are looking for.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services We Use

To run this site and process enquiries, we rely on a small number of trusted third-party services, each of which processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Formspree — processes and delivers submissions from our Contact and Academy Application forms to our inbox.
  • Google Fonts — loads the typefaces used across this site; loading a font may share your IP address with Google.
  • WhatsApp — our floating chat button opens a conversation in WhatsApp at your choice; any messages you send are handled under WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
  • Disqus — powers the comments section on our Insights articles; if you choose to leave a comment, Disqus may set its own cookies under its privacy policy.
  • tawk.to — measures visitor numbers and page views so we can see how the site is being used. Its chat window is switched off, so there is nothing to interact with; it sets cookies and records your IP address, approximate location, browser and the pages you visit, under tawk.to's own privacy policy.

Data Retention

We keep enquiry and application information for as long as needed to respond to you, deliver a project or course, and meet our accounting, tax or legal record-keeping obligations. If you'd like us to delete information we hold about you sooner, contact us and we'll action it unless we're required to retain it by law.

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not share your personal information with third parties except: the service providers listed above (strictly to operate this site and process your submissions), or where required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Frisky Designs or others.

Your Rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of the personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@friskydesigns.com and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for individuals who are at least 18 years old, or who are old enough to enter into a course enrolment or project agreement in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

International Visitors

We're based in India and primarily handle information in accordance with Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. If you're contacting us from outside India, you understand your information will be processed in India.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational or regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, reach out to us at info@friskydesigns.com or +91-7017306096.
