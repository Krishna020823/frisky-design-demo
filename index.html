Our Process

How an idea becomes
a brand.

Discovery
Strategy
Design
Refinement
Delivery
Growth Support

  1. Discovery

    We learn the business first: who buys, who you're up against, and what the brand has to fix.

  2. Strategy

    What we learn becomes a clear position and a direction for the design.

  3. Design

    A few distinct routes, shown to you early, then one taken all the way.

  4. Refinement

    Your feedback, print proofs and real screens decide the final details.

  5. Delivery

    Everything handed over, organised and ready for your printer, developer or marketplace.

  6. Growth Support

    We keep designing after launch, so the brand stays consistent as it grows.
