Attain Your Business to Greater Heights with the Power of Branding

Branding November 10, 2020 By Diksha Babbar Published by Shubham Yadav
With everything that is just a 'click' away, with the convergence of economies, and with the digital revolution, it is important to understand the necessity of branding in order to achieve greater heights. Because in this digital era, "a brand without digital presence is like a bulb without electricity."

How can we help you and why do you need it?

Regardless of the industry, logo designing is the essence of your brand and certainly the first point of contact with the outside world. It is the epicenter of the branding process. As a designer, the aim is to understand the crux of the brand and transform it into shapes and colors that are more likely to instigate a reaction in the viewer. A visual appearance plays a critical role in establishing a brand picture, regardless of the scale a company is working at. A logo needs to be carefully designed according to the personality and the values of your brand.

Logo defines and identifies

A logo should be memorable enough to not get mixed in the crowd, and yet not flashy! It's like playing around with colors and shapes, until there is a winner.

Additionally, a website is another important component of digital marketing as it acts as a digital storefront for your brand. As the speed of interconnectedness through the internet accelerates, the world is becoming a closer, more global economy where websites serve the purpose of giving access to all kinds of information. For example, with the launch of Apple products in 2020, customers were inclined to browse their website to understand the specifications of the products and the new developments in the technology.

Another important pointer about digital presence is portraying your brand in the right way — and that happens when the power of brand messaging is used to its full capacity. It's important to convey the message to the customer in a way they can relate to.

For example:
Nike: Just do it!
Subway: Eat fresh.
Levi's: Quality never goes out of style.

Catchy taglines and stringing words together isn't all that brand messaging is about. To portray your brand well, it's important to understand how your buyer thinks and what would attract them. For example, Cadbury portrays its chocolates as a symbol of bonding and love, with adverts that revolve around the idea of sharing moments of love.

In this fast-paced world of globalizing economies, and this digital shift from offline to online presence, it's important to focus on social media branding. From baby boomers to millennials, everyone has a social media presence — so if your customers are there, your brand needs to be there too. Instagram, Snapchat, even Yelp: it's necessary to identify your audience on these platforms in order to build your brand's presence.

Dig into digital branding — with us today.

By Diksha Babbar
